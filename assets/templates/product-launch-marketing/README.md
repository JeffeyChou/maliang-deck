# Product Launch Marketing

## Use cases and case summary
Auraloop AR Earbuds Launch supports consumer electronics product launch marketing for retail partners, press, and launch marketing leads. A launch team introduces AR earbuds with spatial audio, creator workflows, and retail conversion proof points.

## Typography
Use Sora for headlines, Inter for body copy, and JetBrains Mono for metric labels or code-like annotations.

## Palette and color system
Primary #111827, secondary #FFF7ED, accent #F97316, and neutral #6B7280. Use accent sparingly for Auraloop signals.

## Component and material system
The system includes hero bands, evidence cards, metric charts, decision tables, and three-pillar operating model cards.

## Table and chart styling
Charts use the accent color for priority bars. Tables use high-contrast headers and concise cells tied to Auraloop, earbuds, spatial, retail.

## Icon and shape system
Prefer simple rounded rectangles, thin separators, small badges, and geometric markers over decorative illustration.

## Spacing and alignment
Use a strong outer margin, a consistent title band, and aligned card grids. Keep dense content inside dashboard or table regions.

## Animation / FX / Static fallback
HTML may use element animation, slide FX, and runtime transitions. Static SVG/PDF/PPTX exports ignore motion and preserve the final readable layout.

## Design guidelines
Lead with the case decision, pair every metric with an interpretation, and keep each slide anchored in consumer electronics product launch marketing.

## Dos and don’ts
Do use concrete domain words such as Auraloop, earbuds, spatial, retail, creator, launch. Don’t use generic filler, vague placeholder copy, or ungrounded claims.
